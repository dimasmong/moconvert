GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?+

No. Conversion and ZIP creation happen in your browser. No image files are sent to MoConvert servers.

Can I convert HEIC, TIFF, or AVIF?+

Inputs depend on your browser's decoder. AVIF output also needs browser encoder support. Unsupported files show an honest error instead of fake conversions.

What happens to animated GIFs?+

Animation cannot be preserved by this version. You must explicitly allow first-frame conversion before processing animated images.

Will image quality always stay identical?+

No. JPEG, WebP and AVIF can use lossy encoding, and resizing discards pixels. PNG preserves canvas pixels but metadata and color profiles may change.
